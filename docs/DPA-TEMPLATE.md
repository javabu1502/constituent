# Data Processing Addendum

**This is a draft for counsel review. It has not been reviewed by an attorney.**

---

This Data Processing Addendum ("Addendum") is entered into between:

**My Democracy LLC**, a Nevada limited liability company ("Provider"), and

**______________________** ("Customer").

Effective as of ____________, this Addendum supplements and forms part of the
agreement between the parties governing Customer's use of the My Democracy
platform (the "Agreement"). Where this Addendum conflicts with the Agreement on
the processing of Customer Data, this Addendum controls.

---

## 1. Definitions

**"Customer Data"** means information submitted to the platform by or on behalf
of Customer, and information submitted by a Participant through a campaign
operated by Customer.

**"Participant"** means an individual who takes action through a campaign
operated by Customer.

**"Personal Data"** means information relating to an identified or identifiable
individual contained within Customer Data.

**"Process"** means any operation performed on Personal Data, including
collection, storage, use, disclosure, and deletion.

**"Subprocessor"** means a third party engaged by Provider that Processes
Personal Data.

**"Delivery Record"** means the record of a message transmitted to a government
office through the platform, including the sender's identifying information as
required by that office, the message content, the recipient office, the
transmission method, and the timestamp and status of transmission.

---

## 2. Roles and scope

Customer is the controller of Personal Data it submits and of Personal Data
submitted by Participants through its campaigns. Provider Processes that
Personal Data as a processor on Customer's documented instructions.

Provider acts as an independent controller with respect to Delivery Records, as
described in section 4.

Use of the Agreement and the platform constitutes Customer's documented
instruction to Process Personal Data for the purposes set out in section 3.

---

## 3. Permitted use

Provider will Process Personal Data only to:

a. Provide, maintain, and support the platform for Customer.
b. Deliver messages to the government offices a Participant selects.
c. Produce analytics and reporting for Customer about its own campaigns.
d. Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
e. Comply with applicable law.
f. Act on Customer's other written instructions, where lawful and technically
   feasible.

Provider will **not**:

- Sell Personal Data, as "sell" is defined under applicable privacy law.
- Share Personal Data for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- Use Personal Data to train machine learning or artificial intelligence
  models, or permit a Subprocessor to do so.
- Use Personal Data for Provider's own marketing.
- Disclose Personal Data to another customer or organization on the platform
  except where a Participant has given affirmative, revocable consent.
- Combine Personal Data with data from other sources except as needed to
  provide the platform.

## 4. Ownership, and Provider's retained Delivery Records

As between the parties, Customer owns Customer Data. Provider claims no
ownership and acquires no license beyond what section 3 permits.

**Provider retains Delivery Records independently of Customer.** A message sent
through the platform to a government office is a communication between a
Participant and that office. Provider retains the Delivery Record as the
receipt and audit trail for that transmission, including after Customer's
Agreement terminates and after Customer Data is deleted under section 6.
Provider retains Delivery Records in order to:

a. Evidence that a message was transmitted, to whom, and when.
b. Meet the rules of the U.S. House and U.S. Senate Communicating With Congress
   systems and the individual offices served.
c. Enforce rate limits that prevent any single actor from flooding an office.
d. Respond to an inquiry from a Participant or a government office about a
   specific transmission.

Delivery Records are not disclosed to any other customer or organization,
are not sold, are not used for marketing, and are not used to train models.
A Participant may request deletion of their own account and associated data as
described in section 6.

**Customer should raise any conflict between this section and Customer's own
data policy before execution.**

## 5. Confidentiality

Provider will keep Personal Data confidential and will limit access to
personnel who need it to perform Provider's obligations and who are bound by
confidentiality. As of the effective date, production access is limited to the
owner of Provider. Provider will not disclose Personal Data to a government
authority except where legally compelled, and will notify Customer before
disclosure unless prohibited by law.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Provider retains Personal Data for the term of the Agreement.

**On Customer request**, Provider will delete or return Customer Data, at
Customer's election, within 30 days.

**On termination**, Provider will delete Customer Data within 90 days, except
Delivery Records retained under section 4 and data Provider must retain by law.

**On Participant request**, a Participant may delete their account and
associated data through the platform at any time, or by contacting Provider.

Residual copies in routine backups are deleted on the normal backup expiration
cycle. While they persist, they remain subject to this Addendum.

## 7. Security

Provider will maintain technical and organizational measures appropriate to the
risk, including at minimum:

a. Encryption of Personal Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest
   (AES-256).
b. Row level security on every database table holding Personal Data, with
   application access through a privileged server side path not exposed to the
   browser.
c. Access control limiting production access to authorized personnel.
d. Storage of Personal Data and backups within the United States.
e. Automated daily backups.
f. Automated testing of authorization controls on every change.
g. Rate limiting and bot protection against automated abuse.
h. Periodic internal security review.

Provider will not materially decrease the overall security of the platform
during the term.

A current description of these measures is maintained in Provider's Security
and Privacy Overview, which Provider will furnish on request.

## 8. Breach notification

Provider will notify Customer of a confirmed Personal Data breach affecting
Customer Data **without undue delay and no later than 72 hours** after becoming
aware of it.

The notice will include, to the extent known: the nature of the breach, the
categories and approximate number of records involved, the likely consequences,
the measures taken or proposed, and a contact for further information. Provider
will supplement the notice as the investigation develops and will reasonably
cooperate with Customer's own notification obligations.

## 9. Subprocessors

Customer authorizes Provider to engage Subprocessors. The current list is
published in Provider's Privacy Policy and in section 6 of Provider's Security
and Privacy Overview.

Provider will impose data protection obligations on each Subprocessor no less
protective than this Addendum, and remains liable for a Subprocessor's
performance.

Provider will give Customer **30 days notice** before adding or replacing a
Subprocessor that Processes Personal Data. If Customer reasonably objects on
data protection grounds within that period, the parties will work in good faith
toward a resolution, and failing that Customer may terminate the Agreement for
the affected service without penalty.

## 10. Participant rights

Provider will, on Customer's request and at no charge, assist Customer in
responding to a Participant's request to access, correct, delete, or port their
Personal Data, to the extent Customer cannot do so through the platform.

Where a Participant contacts Provider directly, Provider will either handle the
request through the platform's self-service controls or refer the Participant
to Customer, and will inform Customer.

## 11. Audit

On Customer's written request, no more than once in any twelve month period,
Provider will respond to a reasonable security questionnaire and provide
documentation of the measures in section 7.

Provider does not currently hold a SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 report. If
Provider obtains one, it will be made available in satisfaction of this
section.

## 12. International transfers

Provider stores and Processes Personal Data in the United States and does not
transfer it outside the United States. If that changes, Provider will notify
Customer in advance and put a lawful transfer mechanism in place.

## 13. Liability and term

Each party's liability under this Addendum is subject to the limitations of
liability in the Agreement.

This Addendum takes effect on the effective date and continues for the term of
the Agreement, and thereafter for as long as Provider retains any Personal
Data.
