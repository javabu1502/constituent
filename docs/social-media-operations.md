## Operating mode (authorized 2026-07-21)

Autonomous posting is ON. Originals and citizen replies publish live automatically, subject to the hard guardrails (verified/sourced, nonpartisan, coverage-match, skip discipline, no near-duplicates), cadence, circuit breaker, and the kill switch. Replies to elected officials remain human-gated. This supersedes any earlier 'replies queued for approval' default. The full voice/reply playbook lives at src/lib/social/brand-brain.md.
