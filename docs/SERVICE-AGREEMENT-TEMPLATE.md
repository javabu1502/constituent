# Platform Services Agreement

This Agreement is entered into between:

**My Democracy LLC**, a Nevada limited liability company ("Provider"), and

**______________________** ("Customer").

Effective as of ____________.

---

## 1. What Provider supplies

Provider operates My Democracy, a web platform that lets an organization run
advocacy campaigns. Customer's account includes:

a. Unlimited advocacy campaigns and actions.
b. Delivery of constituent messages to federal offices through the House and
   Senate Communicating With Congress systems, and to state and local offices
   by the method each office publishes.
c. Storytelling campaigns, including consent tracking and story management.
d. Participant and delivery reporting, and data export.
e. Email updates to an organization's own participants.
f. Support by email, with a response on business days.

Provider may change how a feature works, and will give Customer notice before
removing one Customer is using.

## 2. Setup services

Provider will build Customer's first three campaigns with Customer at no
additional charge, including bill research, targeting, and talking points.

Additional campaign-building work is billed at $150 per hour, agreed in
writing before the work begins. Customer can always build its own campaigns
in the platform at no charge.

## 3. Term

This Agreement runs for twelve months from the Effective Date. It renews for
successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives written notice at
least thirty days before the end of a term.

## 4. Fees

Provider's standard fee is **$2,400 per twelve-month term**.

The fee for Customer's first term is **$__________**, invoiced on the
Effective Date and payable within thirty days. Where that figure is a reduced
pilot organization rate, it applies to the first term only.

The fee for each term after the first is Provider's standard fee as of the
renewal date. Provider will give at least sixty days' written notice before a
renewal of the fee that will apply.

Fees are not refundable, except as stated in section 11.

## 5. Customer's responsibilities

Customer is responsible for:

a. The content of its campaigns, including the accuracy of what it tells
   participants about legislation.
b. Having the right to contact anyone it uploads or invites.
c. Its own compliance with lobbying registration and reporting rules, and
   with election law. Provider does not advise on either.
d. Keeping account credentials secure, and telling Provider promptly if an
   account is compromised.

Customer will not use the platform to send unlawful, knowingly false, or
harassing content, or to impersonate a person or organization.

## 6. Data

Each party's obligations for personal data are set out in the Data Processing
Addendum, which forms part of this Agreement. Where this Agreement and the
Addendum conflict on the handling of personal data, the Addendum controls.

Customer's campaign content and participant data belong to Customer. Provider
does not sell Customer Data and does not share it with another organization
except as the Addendum describes.

Customer may export its data at any time while the account is active, and for
thirty days after this Agreement ends.

## 7. Ownership

Provider owns the platform, its software, and its design. Nothing here
transfers any of that to Customer.

Customer owns its campaign content, its name and marks, and its participant
data. Customer grants Provider the limited right to use them to operate the
service for Customer.

## 8. Naming Customer publicly

Provider will not name Customer as a user of the platform, or use Customer's
logo, in any public material without Customer's written permission. Permission
can be withdrawn.

## 9. What Provider does not promise

Provider delivers messages to the systems each government office operates.
Provider does not control those systems and cannot guarantee that an office
accepts, reads, or responds to a message. Provider reports the delivery status
each system returns.

The platform is provided as is. Provider does not warrant that it will be
uninterrupted or error free.

## 10. Limit of liability

Neither party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, or
consequential damages.

Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid
by Customer in the twelve months before the claim.

Nothing in this section limits liability for a party's willful misconduct, or
for Provider's obligations under the Data Processing Addendum.

## 11. Ending this Agreement

Either party may end this Agreement for a material breach the other party has
not fixed within thirty days of written notice.

If Provider stops operating the platform during a term, Provider will give
Customer at least sixty days' notice, help Customer export its data, and
refund the unused portion of the fee.

On termination, Provider will delete Customer Data as set out in the Data
Processing Addendum.

## 12. General

This Agreement, with the Data Processing Addendum, is the entire agreement
between the parties on its subject, and replaces any earlier understanding.

Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Nevada. The parties
submit to the courts of Clark County, Nevada.

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent,
except to a successor to substantially all of its business.
